To solve this problem, we will follow a step-by-step process to determine the number of students in each category and then calculate the final percentage.

**Step 1: Identify the given values.**
- Total number of students: $290$
- Students who like to paint: $12$
- Students who like to play chess: $8$
- Students who like to swim: $13$
- Students who like to play cricket: $39$

**Step 2: Calculate the number of students who like to hike.**
The problem states that the number of students who like to hike is 5 less than two times the number that prefer playing cricket.
- Number of cricket players = $39$
- Two times the number of cricket players = $2 \times 39 = 78$
- Number of hikers = $78 - 5 = 73$

**Step 3: Calculate the number of students who like to play video games.**
The "rest" of the students (after accounting for painting, chess, swimming, cricket, and hiking) like to play video games.
First, let's sum the students accounted for so far:
- Total accounted = $12$ (paint) + $8$ (chess) + $13$ (swim) + $39$ (cricket) + $73$ (hike)
- Total accounted = $145$

Now, subtract this sum from the total number of students to find the number of video game players:
- Video game players = $290 - 145 = 145$

**Step 4: Calculate the percentage of students who like to play video games.**
To find the percentage, we use the formula: $\text{Percentage} = \left( \frac{\text{Part}}{\text{Whole}} \right) \times 100$
- Part (video game players) = $145$
- Whole (total students) = $290$
- Percentage = $\left( \frac{145}{290} \right) \times 100$

Since $145$ is exactly half of $290$ ($145 \times 2 = 290$):
- $\frac{145}{290} = 0.5$
- $0.5 \times 100 = 50\%$

Final answer: 50
