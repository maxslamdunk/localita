To find the number of forks in the package Valentina bought, we can set up an equation based on the information provided.

**Step 2: Set up the equation.**
The total number of forks is the sum of the initial amount plus the additions, minus the subtractions.
The sequence of events is:
1. Valentina buys $x$ forks.
2. Husband adds $19$ forks.
3. Valentina uses $6$ forks (these are removed from the "new" pool/available count).
4. Valentina throws away $8$ forks.
5. Guests bring $16$ forks ($2 \times 8$).
6. The final total is $49$.

Equation:
$x + 19 - 6 - 8 + 16 = 49$

**Step 3: Solve the equation.**
First, simplify the constants on the left side:
$19 - 6 = 13$
$13 - 8 = 5$
$5 + 16 = 21$

So the equation becomes:
$x + 21 = 49$

Subtract 21 from both sides:
$x = 49 - 21$
$x = 28$

- Add 19: $28 + 19 = 47$
