To find the difference between the number of apricots and grapes harvested in two years, we calculate the total production for each crop step by step.

### 1. Calculate Apricot Production
**Field Details:**
*   Total area: 20 hectares
*   Yield rate: 28 apricots per two-fourths (0.5) of a hectare.
    *   Yield per hectare = $28 \times 2 = 56$ apricots/hectare.
*   Harvest frequency: Every 6 months.

**Total Harvests in 2 years:**
*   2 years = 24 months.
*   Number of harvests = $24 / 6 = 4$ harvests.

**Total Apricots:**
*   Apricots per harvest = $20 \text{ hectares} \times 56 \text{ apricots/hectare} = 1120$ apricots.
*   Total apricots in 2 years = $1120 \times 4 = 4480$ apricots.

### 2. Calculate Grape Production
**Field Details:**
*   Total area: 12 hectares
*   Yield rate: 14 grapes per hectare.
*   Harvest frequency: Every 4 months.

**Total Harvests in 2 years:**
*   2 years = 24 months.
*   Number of harvests = $24 / 4 = 6$ harvests.

**Total Grapes:**
*   Grapes per harvest = $12 \text{ hectares} \times 14 \text{ grapes/hectare} = 168$ grapes.
*   Total grapes in 2 years = $168 \times 6 = 1008$ grapes.

### 3. Calculate the Difference
*   Difference = Total Apricots - Total Grapes
*   Difference = $4480 - 1008 = 3472$

Final answer: 3472
