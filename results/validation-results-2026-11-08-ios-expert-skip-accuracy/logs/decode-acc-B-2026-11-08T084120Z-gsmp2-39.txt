To solve this problem, we need to calculate the total time spent cleaning and compare it to the total time available.

**Step 1: Determine the new time per room.**
The original time per room was 6 minutes. Because the janitor is now twice as slow, the new time per room is:
$6 \text{ minutes} \times 2 = 12 \text{ minutes per room}$.

**Step 2: Calculate the total cleaning time for all rooms.**
There are 80 rooms in total.
$\text{Total cleaning time} = 80 \text{ rooms} \times 12 \text{ minutes/room} = 960 \text{ minutes}$.

**Step 3: Calculate the total available working time.**
The janitor works for 4 days, and each day consists of 10 hours.
$\text{Total available time} = 4 \text{ days} \times 10 \text{ hours/day} = 40 \text{ hours}$.
To compare this with the cleaning time, we convert the hours into minutes:
$40 \text{ hours} \times 60 \text{ minutes/hour} = 2400 \text{ minutes}$.

**Step 4: Calculate the percentage of the day spent cleaning.**
The percentage is the ratio of the total cleaning time to the total available time, multiplied by 100.
$\text{Percentage} = \left( \frac{\text{Total cleaning time}}{\text{Total available time}} \right) \times 100$
$\text{Percentage} = \left( \frac{960}{2400} \right) \times 100$

Simplify the fraction:
$\frac{960}{2400} = \frac{96}{240}$
Divide both numerator and denominator by 24:
$96 \div 24 = 4$
$240 \div 24 = 10$
So, $\frac{4}{10} = 0.4$.

$\text{Percentage} = 0.4 \times 100 = 40\%$.
