To solve this problem, we will follow these steps:

1.  **Calculate the initial state of the rooms:**
    *   Total capacity = 1700 rooms.
    *   Occupied rooms = $\frac{1}{5} \times 1700 = 340$ rooms.
    *   Out of service rooms = $\frac{1}{5} \times 1700 = 340$ rooms.

2.  **Determine the initial number of available (unoccupied) rooms:**
    The rooms that are not occupied and not out of service are the ones available for use.
    *   Available rooms = Total capacity - Occupied rooms - Out of service rooms
    *   Available rooms = $1700 - 340 - 340 = 1020$ rooms.

3.  **Calculate the number of patients admitted over 2 weeks:**
    *   Time period = 2 weeks = $2 \times 7 = 14$ days.
    *   Daily admission rate = 20 patients per day.
    *   Total patients admitted = $14 \text{ days} \times 20 \text{ patients/day} = 280$ patients.

4.  **Calculate the final number of unoccupied rooms:**
    Since the 280 new patients will occupy the available rooms:
    *   Final unoccupied rooms = Initial available rooms - Total patients admitted
    *   Final unoccupied rooms = $1020 - 280 = 740$ rooms.

Final answer: 740
