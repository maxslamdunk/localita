To find the average age of the four people in 4 years, we first need to determine their current ages and then their ages in 4 years.

*   Sum of ages in 4 years = $25 + 41 + 16 + 26$
*   $25 + 41 = 66$
*   $66 + 16 = 82$
*   $82 + 26 = 108$

*   $108 \div 4 = 27$
