To find the number of forks in the package Valentina bought, we can set up an equation based on the information provided.

**1. Identify the variables and given values:**
*   Let $x$ be the number of forks in the package Valentina bought.
*   Forks from husband: $19$
*   Forks used for sampling: $6$
*   Defective forks thrown away: $8$
*   Forks brought by guests: $2 \times 8 = 16$
*   Final total of forks: $49$

**2. Set up the equation:**
The total number of forks is the sum of the initial amount plus the guest forks, minus the forks used/thrown away.
The sequence of events is:
(Initial package $x$) + (Husband's package $19$) - (Used $6$) - (Defective $8$) + (Guest packs $16$) = $49$

**3. Solve the equation step by step:**
First, simplify the constants on the left side:
$x + 19 - 6 - 8 + 16 = 49$

Combine the numbers:
$19 - 6 = 13$
$13 - 8 = 5$
$5 + 16 = 21$

So, the equation becomes:
$x + 21 = 49$

Now, subtract 21 from both sides to solve for $x$:
$x = 49 - 21$
$x = 28$

$28$ (bought) + $19$ (husband) = $47$
$47 - 6$ (sampled) = $41$
