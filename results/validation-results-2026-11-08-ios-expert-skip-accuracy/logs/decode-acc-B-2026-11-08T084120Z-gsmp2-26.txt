To find the total number of pizzas eaten in two months, we calculate the consumption for each month step by step.

**Step 1: Calculate the daily consumption in the first month.**
- Uncle's consumption: 16 pizzas/day
- Spouse's consumption: 16 pizzas/day
- Combined consumption (Uncle + Spouse): $16 + 16 = 32$ pizzas/day
- Grandchild's consumption: $\frac{1}{2} \times 32 = 16$ pizzas/day
- Total daily consumption for the family: $32 + 16 = 48$ pizzas/day

**Step 2: Calculate the total consumption for the first month.**
Assuming a standard month of 30 days (as is common in such word problems unless specified otherwise):
- Total pizzas in Month 1: $48 \text{ pizzas/day} \times 30 \text{ days} = 1440$ pizzas.

**Step 3: Calculate the daily consumption for the second month.**
The second month is divided into two halves (15 days each).
- **First half (Days 1-15):**
  The consumption is reduced by 25%.
  New daily rate = $48 \times (1 - 0.25) = 48 \times 0.75 = 36$ pizzas/day.
  Total for first half: $36 \text{ pizzas/day} \times 15 \text{ days} = 540$ pizzas.

- **Second half (Days 16-30):**
  The consumption is reduced by an *additional* 50% from the current rate (36 pizzas/day).
  New daily rate = $36 \times (1 - 0.50) = 18$ pizzas/day.
  Total for second half: $18 \text{ pizzas/day} \times 15 \text{ days} = 270$ pizzas.

- **Total for Month 2:** $540 + 270 = 810$ pizzas.

**Step 4: Calculate the total consumption for both months.**
- Total = Month 1 + Month 2
- Total = $1440 + 810 = 2250$ pizzas.

Final answer: 2250
