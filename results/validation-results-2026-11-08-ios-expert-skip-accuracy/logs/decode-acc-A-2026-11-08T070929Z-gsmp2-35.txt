To find out how much money Thiago is still missing, we need to calculate the total cost, subtract the amounts covered, and determine the remaining balance.

**Step 1: Calculate the total cost of the science fair.**
The science fair lasts for 3 days. The costs per day are:
- Hotel: $250
- Food: $30
- Transportation: $30

Total cost per day = $250 + $30 + $30 = $310.
Total cost for 3 days = $310 * 3 = $930.

**Step 2: Subtract the food costs covered by friends.**
The friends cover all the food costs.
Total food cost = $30 * 3 = $90.
Remaining cost after friends' contribution = $930 - $90 = $840.

**Step 3: Calculate the school's contribution.**
The school covers 1/4 of the remaining cost ($840).
School contribution = $840 * (1/4) = $210.
Remaining cost after school's contribution = $840 - $210 = $630.

**Step 4: Subtract Thiago's own money to find the missing amount.**
Thiago has $35.
Missing amount = $630 - $35 = $595.

**Summary of calculations:**
- Total cost: $930
- Food (covered): $90
- Remaining: $840
- School (1/4 of $840): $210
- Remaining: $630
- Thiago's money: $35
- Final missing amount: $630 - $35 = $595.
