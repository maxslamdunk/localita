To find out how many tickets each graduate receives, we need to calculate the remaining available seats and then divide that number by the number of graduates.

**Step 1: Calculate the total number of reserved seats.**
The reserved seats are for the graduates, the faculty, and the staff.
- Graduates: 600 seats
- Faculty: 250 seats
- Staff: 50 seats

Total reserved seats = $600 + 250 + 50 = 900$ seats.

**Step 2: Calculate the remaining available seats.**
The total capacity of the Sports Arena is 7500. We subtract the reserved seats from the total capacity to find the seats available for friends and family.
- Total capacity: 7500
- Reserved seats: 900

Available seats = $7500 - 900 = 6600$ seats.

**Step 3: Calculate how many tickets each graduate receives.**
The 6600 available seats are to be split equally among the 600 graduates.
- Available seats: 6600
- Number of graduates: 600

Tickets per graduate = $6600 / 600$

To simplify the division:
$6600 / 600 = 66 / 6 = 11$

Each graduate receives 11 tickets.

Final answer: 11
