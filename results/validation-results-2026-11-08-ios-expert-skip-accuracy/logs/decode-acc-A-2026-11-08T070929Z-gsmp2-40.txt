To find out how much money Sakura has left, we need to calculate her total expenses and subtract them from her initial amount.

**Step 1: Calculate the cost of the saltwater taffy.**
The sale offer is: "Buy 16 kgs at €22 per kg, get 4 kgs 1/4 off."
Sakura bought 22 kgs in total. Let's break down the pricing based on the offer:
- The first 16 kgs are at the full price: $16 \times 22 = €352$.
- The next 4 kgs are 1/4 off. 
  - Discount per kg: $22 \times \frac{1}{4} = 5.50$.
  - Sale price per kg: $22 - 5.50 = 16.50$.
  - Cost for these 4 kgs: $4 \times 16.50 = €66$.
- The remaining kgs (since she bought 22 kgs total: $22 - 16 - 4 = 2$ kgs) would be at the standard price of €22 per kg (as the discount only applied to the 4 kgs following the initial 16).
  - Cost for the remaining 2 kgs: $2 \times 22 = €44$.

Total cost for taffy: $352 + 66 + 44 = €462$.

**Step 2: Calculate the cost of other items.**
- Mixed bag of starfish: €11.0
- 12 keychains at €20.0 each: $12 \times 20 = €240$.
- Charity donation: €5.

**Step 3: Calculate total expenses.**
Total Expenses = Taffy + Starfish + Keychains + Donation
Total Expenses = $462 + 11 + 240 + 5$
Total Expenses = $718$.

**Step 4: Calculate the remaining money.**
Initial amount: €1300
Remaining money = $1300 - 718$
Remaining money = $582$.

Final answer: 582
