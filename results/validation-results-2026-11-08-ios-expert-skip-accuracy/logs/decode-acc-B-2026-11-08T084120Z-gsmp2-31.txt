To solve this problem, we will determine the current ages of Ravi, Pavel, and Leilani step by step.

The problem states that Ravi will be 47 in 8 years.
Current age of Ravi = $47 - 8 = 39$.

**Step 2: Find Pavel's current age.**
The problem states that Ravi and Pavel are 22 years apart, and Ravi is younger than Pavel.
Current age of Pavel = $39 + 22 = 61$.

**Step 3: Find Leilani's current age.**
The problem states that Ravi and Leilani are 10 years apart, and Ravi is older than Leilani.
Current age of Leilani = $39 - 10 = 29$.

**Step 4: Calculate the average age of all three.**
The ages are 39, 61, and 29.
Sum of ages = $39 + 61 + 29 = 129$.
Average age = $\frac{129}{3} = 43$.

**Step 5: Calculate how much younger Leilani is than the average age.**
Leilani's age = 29.
Average age = 43.
Difference = $43 - 29 = 14$.
