To find the percentage of animals in the garden that were rabbits, we need to determine the total number of animals and the number of rabbits.

**Step 1: Identify the number of each type of animal.**
*   **Caterpillars:** The problem states there are 13 caterpillars.
*   **Beetles:** There are nine times as many beetles as caterpillars.
    $13 \times 9 = 117$ beetles.
*   **White animals:** There are 66 white animals.
*   **Rabbits:** Half of the white animals are rabbits.
    $66 / 2 = 33$ rabbits.
*   **Bees:** There are 24 bees.

**Step 2: Calculate the total number of animals in the garden.**
The total number of animals is the sum of caterpillars, beetles, white animals, and bees.
*Note: The problem states "66 white animals, half of which were rabbits." This implies the rabbits are a subset of the white animals. We must be careful not to double-count. The white animals (66) already include the rabbits (33). The other 33 white animals are a different type of animal, but they are still counted in the total population.*

Total animals = Caterpillars + Beetles + White animals + Bees
Total animals = $13 + 117 + 66 + 24$
Total animals = $130 + 66 + 24$
Total animals = $130 + 90$
Total animals = $220$

**Step 3: Calculate the percentage of rabbits.**
The number of rabbits is 33.
The total number of animals is 220.

Percentage of rabbits = $(\text{Number of rabbits} / \text{Total animals}) \times 100$
Percentage of rabbits = $(33 / 220) \times 100$

Simplify the fraction:
$33 / 220 = (3 \times 11) / (20 \times 11) = 3 / 20$

Convert to percentage:
$(3 / 20) \times 100 = 3 \times 5 = 15\%$

Final answer: 15
