To find the difference in cost between 2 loaves of sourdough and 8 muffins, we follow these steps:

1.  **Calculate the cost of one loaf of sourdough:**
    The cost of one loaf is given as $10.

2.  **Calculate the cost of one muffin:**
    The cost of a muffin is one-eighth of a loaf of sourdough.
    $\text{Cost of one muffin} = \frac{1}{8} \times 10 = \$1.25$

3.  **Calculate the cost of 8 muffins with the promotion:**
    The promotion states that if you buy 6 or more muffins, you get a 10% discount on the muffins. Since 8 is greater than 6, the discount applies.
    $\text{Total cost of 8 muffins before discount} = 8 \times 1.25 = \$10.00$
    $\text{Discount amount} = 10\% \text{ of } \$10.00 = 0.10 \times 10 = \$1.00$
    $\text{Total cost of 8 muffins after discount} = 10.00 - 1.00 = \$9.00$

4.  **Calculate the cost of 2 loaves of sourdough:**
    $\text{Cost of 2 loaves} = 2 \times 10 = \$20.00$

5.  **Calculate how much more the 2 loaves cost than the 8 muffins:**
    $\text{Difference} = \text{Cost of 2 loaves} - \text{Cost of 8 muffins}$
    $\text{Difference} = 20.00 - 9.00 = \$11.00$

Final answer: 11
