To find the total time it takes Olga to peel, wash, and sauté 180 clams, we need to calculate the time for each individual task and sum them up.

**Step 1: Calculate the time for washing.**
There are 180 clams. Each clam takes 19 seconds to wash.
Total washing time = $180 \times 19$ seconds.
$180 \times 19 = 3420$ seconds.
Convert seconds to minutes: $3420 / 60 = 57$ minutes.

**Step 2: Calculate the time for peeling.**
Olga can peel 12 clams per minute.
Total peeling time = $180 / 12 = 15$ minutes.

**Step 3: Calculate the break time.**
The problem states she takes an 11-minute break after peeling before she can start sautéing.
Break time = 11 minutes.

**Step 4: Calculate the time for sautéing.**
She sautés 30 clams in 9 minutes.
First, find the rate per clam: $9 \text{ minutes} / 30 \text{ clams} = 0.3$ minutes per clam.
Total sautéing time = $180 \times 0.3 = 54$ minutes.
(Alternatively: $180 / 30 = 6$ batches. $6 \times 9 = 54$ minutes.)

**Step 5: Calculate the total time.**
Since she cannot work in parallel, we sum all the sequential tasks:
Total time = Washing time + Peeling time + Break time + Sautéing time
Total time = $57 + 15 + 11 + 54$

Summing them up:
$57 + 15 = 72$
$72 + 11 = 83$
$83 + 54 = 137$

Final check of the steps:
- Washing: $180 \times 19 / 60 = 57$ mins. (Correct)
- Peeling: $180 / 12 = 15$ mins. (Correct)
- Break: 11 mins. (Correct)
- Sautéing: $(180 / 30) \times 9 = 54$ mins. (Correct)
- Total: $57 + 15 + 11 + 54 = 137$ mins. (Correct)

Final answer: 137
