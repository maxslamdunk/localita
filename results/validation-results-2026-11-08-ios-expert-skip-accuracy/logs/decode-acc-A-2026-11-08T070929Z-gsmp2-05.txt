To solve this problem, we define the variables based on the given ratios and the total amount used.

- $s$ = teaspoons of sugar
- $w$ = cups of water
- $m$ = tablespoons of milk
- $c$ = tablespoons of heavy cream

We are given that the total sum of these ingredients is 220:
$s + w + m + c = 220$

Substitute $w$, $m$, and $c$ in terms of $s$:
- $w = \frac{5}{51}s$
- $m = \frac{7}{5}w = \frac{7}{5} \left( \frac{5}{51}s \right) = \frac{7}{51}s$
- $c = 2s$

Now, substitute these into the sum equation:
$s + \frac{5}{51}s + \frac{7}{51}s + 2s = 220$

$(1 + 2)s + \left( \frac{5}{51} + \frac{7}{51} \right)s = 220$
$3s + \frac{12}{51}s = 220$

$\frac{12}{51} = \frac{4}{17}$

So the equation becomes:
$3s + \frac{4}{17}s = 220$

$\frac{51}{17}s + \frac{4}{17}s = 220$
$\frac{55}{17}s = 220$

Solve for $s$:
$55s = 220 \times 17$
$s = \frac{220 \times 17}{55}$

$220 / 55 = 4$
