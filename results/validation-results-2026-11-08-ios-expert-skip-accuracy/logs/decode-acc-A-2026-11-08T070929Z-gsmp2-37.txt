To find out how many days it will take for the pizza to be fully eaten, we first determine the daily consumption rates of each person.

**Step 1: Calculate individual consumption rates.**
*   **Aisha:** 3 servings per day.

**Step 2: Calculate total consumption for the first 2 days.**
The total daily consumption for the first 2 days is:
$\text{Aisha} + \text{Amelia} + \text{Ava} = 3 + 6 + 24 = 33$ servings per day.

Over 2 days, the total amount eaten is:
$33 \text{ servings/day} \times 2 \text{ days} = 66$ servings.

**Step 3: Calculate the remaining servings.**
The total pizza size is 87 servings. After 2 days:
$87 - 66 = 21$ servings remaining.

**Step 4: Calculate the new daily consumption rate.**
After 2 days, Ava cuts her consumption by half:
$\text{New Ava rate} = 24 / 2 = 12$ servings per day.

The new total daily consumption is:
$\text{Aisha} + \text{Amelia} + \text{New Ava} = 3 + 6 + 12 = 21$ servings per day.

**Step 5: Calculate the time to finish the remaining servings.**
The remaining 21 servings are consumed at a rate of 21 servings per day:
$21 \text{ servings} / 21 \text{ servings/day} = 1$ day.

**Step 6: Calculate the total number of days.**
Total days = (Initial 2 days) + (1 additional day) = 3 days.
