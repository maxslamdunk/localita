**Step 2: Determine the number of each type of cube.**
The problem states there are 47 total cubes. We are given:
- Giant cubes: 9
- Medium cubes: 12
- Small cubes: 14
- Tiny cubes: Total cubes - (Giant + Medium + Small)
  - Tiny cubes = $47 - (9 + 12 + 14) = 47 - 35 = 12$

**Step 3: Calculate the water used for each type of cube.**
- Water for giant cubes: $9 \times 14 = 126$ ounces
- Water for medium cubes: $12 \times 3 = 36$ ounces
- Water for small cubes: $14 \times 0.5 = 7$ ounces
- Water for tiny cubes: $12 \times 0.25 = 3$ ounces

**Step 4: Calculate the total water used.**
Total water used = $126 + 36 + 7 + 3$
- $126 + 36 = 162$
- $162 + 7 = 169$
- $169 + 3 = 172$
Total water used = 172 ounces.

**Step 5: Calculate the remaining water.**
Remaining water = Initial water - Total water used
- Remaining water = $337 - 172 = 165$
