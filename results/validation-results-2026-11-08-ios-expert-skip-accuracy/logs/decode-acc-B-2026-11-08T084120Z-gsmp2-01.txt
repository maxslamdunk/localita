To solve this problem, we need to determine the rate of the fog bank's movement and calculate the time it takes to cover a specific distance.

**1. Determine the initial speed of the fog bank:**
The problem states that it takes 30 minutes to cover 3 miles.
Initial speed ($v_1$) = $\frac{3 \text{ miles}}{30 \text{ minutes}} = 0.1 \text{ miles per minute}$.

**2. Determine the target distance:**
The city is 72 miles across. We need to find how long it takes to cover a quarter of the city.
Target distance = $\frac{1}{4} \times 72 \text{ miles} = 18 \text{ miles}$.

**3. Account for the change in wind speed:**
The wind speed (which dictates the fog speed) is halved in one hour (60 minutes).
New speed ($v_2$) = $\frac{0.1 \text{ miles per minute}}{2} = 0.05 \text{ miles per minute}$.

**4. Calculate the progress made in the first hour:**
In the first 60 minutes, the fog bank travels at $v_1 = 0.1$ miles per minute.
Distance covered in 60 minutes = $60 \text{ minutes} \times 0.1 \text{ miles/minute} = 6 \text{ miles}$.

**5. Calculate the remaining distance to reach the target:**
The target distance is 18 miles.
Remaining distance = $18 \text{ miles} - 6 \text{ miles} = 12 \text{ miles}$.

**6. Calculate the time required to cover the remaining distance:**
After the first hour, the speed becomes $v_2 = 0.05$ miles per minute.
Time to cover 12 miles = $\frac{12 \text{ miles}}{0.05 \text{ miles/minute}} = 240 \text{ minutes}$.

**7. Calculate the total time:**
Total time = Time in first hour + Time for remaining distance
Total time = $60 \text{ minutes} + 240 \text{ minutes} = 300 \text{ minutes}$.

Final answer: 300
