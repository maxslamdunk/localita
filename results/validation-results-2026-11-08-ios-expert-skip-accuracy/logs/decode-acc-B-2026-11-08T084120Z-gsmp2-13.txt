To find out how many potted plants Sakura will have remaining, we need to calculate the total number of plants she has and then subtract the number of plants she gives away.

Sakura has 60 window ledges, and each ledge has 7 plants.

She received 49 new plants.
$420 + 49 = 469$ plants.

She purchased 100 new plants yesterday. (Note: The 50% discount is a detail about the price, not the quantity, so we add the full 100 plants).
$469 + 100 = 569$ plants.

**Step 4: Calculate how many plants she will give away.**
She decides to give 3 plants from each of her 60 ledges.
$60 \times 3 = 180$ plants.

**Step 5: Calculate the remaining number of plants.**
Subtract the plants given away from the total count.
