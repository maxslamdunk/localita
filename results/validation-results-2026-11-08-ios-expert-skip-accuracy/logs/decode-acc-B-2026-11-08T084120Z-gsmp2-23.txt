To find out how much Sakura should pay, we need to calculate the total number of skeins required, the total cost before the discount, and then apply the discount.

**Step 1: Calculate the wool needed for the grandchildren.**
There are 4 grandchildren. For each child, Sakura makes:
- 1 vest: 5 skeins
- 1 cardigan: 4 skeins
- 1 shawl: 10 skeins
- 1 pair of gloves: 12 skeins
- 1 pair of mittens: 9 skeins

Total skeins per child = $5 + 4 + 10 + 12 + 9 = 40$ skeins.
Total skeins for 4 children = $40 \times 4 = 160$ skeins.

**Step 2: Calculate the wool needed for Sakura.**
Sakura wants to make 2 jackets.
- 1 jacket: 4 skeins
Total skeins for Sakura = $2 \times 4 = 8$ skeins.

**Step 3: Calculate the total number of skeins.**
Total skeins = $160 + 8 = 168$ skeins.

**Step 4: Calculate the total cost.**
The wool is sold by the dozen. One dozen is 12 skeins.
Total dozens = $168 / 12 = 14$ dozens.
The cost per dozen is $9.
Total cost before discount = $14 \times 9 = \$126$.

**Step 5: Apply the promotion.**
The store offers a $20 discount on purchases over $100.
Since $126 is greater than $100, Sakura is eligible for the discount.
Final price = $\$126 - \$20 = \$106$.
