### Difficulties in Translation

**1. "The Great Stink"**
*   **Why:** In English, "The Great Stink" is a punchy, evocative title. Translating it literally as *"La Grande Odeur"* would be incorrect (as *odeur* is neutral/positive), and *"La Grande Puanteur"* sounds slightly more archaic or heavy. The challenge was finding a term that captured the historical gravity of the event while maintaining the descriptive nature of the English noun phrase.

**2. "The miasma from the effluent was thought to transmit..."**
*   **Why:** This phrase involves a combination of archaic medical terminology (*miasma*) and a passive construction (*was thought to*). In French, a literal translation like *"Le miasme était pensé pour..."* is grammatically incorrect. I had to use the impersonal construction *"On pensait que..."* to maintain the formal, historical tone while ensuring the sentence flowed naturally in French syntax.
